What is figure skating?

Figure skating combines balance, strength, and creativity. Skaters move to music using jumps, spins, and steps.

A great performance brings technique and expression together.

Three Essential Elements

Jumps

Skaters take off, rotate in the air, and land on one foot. Timing and control make the landing smooth.

Spins

Skaters rotate in one place while holding different positions. Balance helps them stay centered.

Steps and Turns

Footwork connects the elements of a program. Changes of direction and rhythm bring the music to life.
